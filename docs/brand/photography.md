# Photography — what to send, and what happens when you do

The site currently has **zero photographs**. That is the largest remaining gap
in it. The pitch is *local, bilingual, one person you can actually reach* — and
there is no face anywhere on the site to attach that to.

The slots are already built. Each one renders **nothing** until its file exists,
so nothing is broken while we wait and nothing placeholder-looking ever ships.
Adding a photo is: drop the file in with the exact name below, commit, push.
No code change.

---

## Slot 1 — Your portrait (do this one first)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Save as** | `public/photos/dan-lopez.jpg` |
| **Size** | 800 × 1000 px (4:5 portrait), under ~300 KB |
| **Appears** | `/about`, beside your name and quote, in both languages |
| **Alt text** | Already written and translated — you don't need to supply it |

**How to shoot it.** A phone in portrait mode is genuinely fine; a bad studio
photo looks worse than a good phone one. What matters:

- **Window light, facing you.** Stand a couple of feet from a window, face it.
  Never with the window behind you.
- **Uncluttered background.** A plain wall, a workspace, anything outdoors that
  is not busy. Leave breathing room around your head — we crop to 4:5.
- **Look at the lens.** Chest-up. Wear what you would wear to meet a client at
  their office, which for the Valley is not a suit.
- **No heavy filters, no fake blur.** People read those as stock.

Take twenty and pick one. The good one is usually not the one you posed for.

## Slot 2 — Workspace / on-site (worth doing, not urgent)

Three or four landscape shots, 1600 × 1000 px: your desk mid-work, screens with
something real on them, you with a client if anyone will consent to it. These
are what a prospect scans to decide you are a real operation.

Send them and I will build the slot to match what the photos actually are —
designing the layout before seeing them is how sites end up with three
identical grey rectangles.

**Anything with a client, a client's screen, or a client's paperwork in it needs
their explicit say-so first.** A recognizable invoice or patient list in the
background of a photo is a real problem, not a hypothetical one.

## What a photo unlocks beyond the page

- **Google Business Profile** ranks listings with photos higher, and a face
  performs better than a logo for a solo consultancy.
- **LinkedIn and link previews** — the OG card is currently type-only.
- **Image search** is a channel the site has no presence in at all today.

## Brand assets, if you need the mark elsewhere

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `public/brand/bis-mark.svg` | Vector. Printers, signage, anything that scales. |
| `https://bis-rgv.com/brand/mark.png` | 512 × 512 raster. LinkedIn, Google Business Profile, invoice templates, anywhere that rejects SVG. |
| `https://bis-rgv.com/icon.svg` | What browsers use for the tab. Don't hand this one out; it is the same art, wired for a different job. |

The mark is the chevron from the **bis&gt;** wordmark on a violet-to-teal tile.
Keep clear space around it of about a quarter of its width, and don't recolour
it — the gradient is the brand, and on a dark background the tile already holds
its own edge.
