Every AI vendor has a demo. The demo is rehearsed, it runs on a sandbox that gets reset between meetings, and it stops the moment you ask something off-script. What it proves is that the vendor can build a demo.

## So we put ours on our own number

The number at the top of this site is answered by Sofía, an AI receptionist we built. Not a sandbox — the real line, the one a prospect or a client dials. She works in English and Spanish, follows you if you switch languages mid-sentence, and if you ask for a free assessment she checks the calendar and books it while you are still on the call.

If she gets something wrong, she gets it wrong in front of a customer. That was the point of putting her there.

## What "shipped" has to mean

Something is not shipped because it worked in a meeting. It is shipped when it carries weight — when something you care about breaks if it fails. For us, three things had to be true before she answered a single call:

- Every call leaves a record: a transcript, a summary, and a lead in the same database our contact form writes to.
- A booking is a real booking, on the real calendar, with the confirmation email the customer expects.
- If anything fails after the appointment is made, the appointment still stands. Taking back a customer's slot to cover our own error is not an option.

## The test you should apply to us

Ask any vendor — us included — to show you something running that you can try yourself, without their hand on the wheel. Then actually try it. [Our number is on the work page](/en/work), and nobody is standing by to catch it.
